# Reddit routine

The product is **{{PRODUCT}}** ({{PRODUCT_URL}}): {{ONE_LINE_WHAT_IT_DOES}}.

## Reply rules

- Answer the question first and completely.
- Mention {{PRODUCT}} only when it is genuinely the answer, and say I work on it.
- No links unless the person asked for one.
- Under 120 words. No bullet points. No marketing words.
- Never DM anyone. Never post the same reply twice.
- I post everything by hand, from my own account. Draft, never post.

## Voice

Plain, specific, first person. The kind of reply I would leave under my own
name whether or not I had anything to sell.
